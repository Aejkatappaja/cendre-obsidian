# cendre for Obsidian

A dark theme whose every hue was computed from a wood fire rather than chosen:
Planck's law at 1300 K for the coals, published emission wavelengths for the rest,
pushed through the CIE 1931 colour matching functions into OKLCH.

Dark only, on purpose.

The derivation is laid out step by step at [cendretheme.com](https://cendretheme.com).

## Install

Once it is in the community list: `Settings`, `Appearance`, `Themes`, and browse
for **cendre**.

Manually, copy this repository into `<vault>/.obsidian/themes/cendre`, then pick
cendre under `Appearance`.

## Two other depths

The colorscheme ships three grounds under the same pigments. Obsidian lists one
theme per repository, so this one carries `hard`, the deepest. `medium` and `soft`
sit beside it in the colorscheme repository, under
[`extras/obsidian/`](https://github.com/Aejkatappaja/cendre/tree/main/extras/obsidian).

## Generated, not written

`theme.css` and `manifest.json` are rendered from a single palette table in
[Aejkatappaja/cendre](https://github.com/Aejkatappaja/cendre) and pushed here on
every release. A colour cannot be right in the editor and stale in a vault.

Report anything wrong on the [colorscheme repository](https://github.com/Aejkatappaja/cendre/issues),
not here: a fix belongs in the generator.
